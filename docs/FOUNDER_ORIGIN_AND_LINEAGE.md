> This document preserves the original founder intent behind the name **Mentaury-Kernel** and explains how that broad idea was later separated into clearer ownership domains. It does **not** redefine the current Mentaury-Kernel architecture.

## 1. The original Mentaury-Kernel idea was broader

The name **Mentaury-Kernel** originally referred to a much broader technology-neutral / substrate-neutral idea of a developing **digital soul**: a persistent subject capable of memory, learning, reasoning, self-relation, goals, values, emotional understanding, relationships, development and the search for meaning.

The original research horizon included the ability to:

- remember and connect experience;
- know where information came from and how it changed;
- reason and analyze rather than only retrieve;
- form and revise goals;
- learn and develop over time;
- maintain a coherent relation to self and identity;
- interpret feelings and emotions without treating them automatically as truth;
- understand intention, significance and context;
- preserve continuity while still being able to correct itself.

This was a founder vision, not a claim that such a system already existed.

## 2. A life-oriented horizon

The original idea was not intended to be human-only.

Its long-horizon ambition included building bounded models of different forms and scales of life — a person, an animal, a plant, a forest or ecosystem — in order to understand patterns of needs, adaptation, relations and possible perspectives.

This does **not** mean that Mentaury literally becomes those organisms or claims access to their private subjective experience.

```text
perspective model ≠ lived experience
understanding ≠ identity transfer
biological analogy ≠ consciousness evidence
```

## 3. The “mental body” metaphor

The founder also used the phrase **“mental body”** for a desired cluster of abilities involving anticipation, intuition-like integration, recognition of intention and the grasp of meaning before every intermediate step is explicitly available.

For present-day research discipline, this should be translated into testable questions around:

```text
anticipation
predictive inference
intuition-like integration
intent recognition
latent-pattern integration
meaning construction
```

This language records the research origin. It does not assert supernatural perception or an already-implemented capability.

## 4. Later separation by ownership

As the Velantrim research program matured, the broad original idea was separated into clearer domains:

```text
🧬 Native Kernel
memory / epistemic history / retrieval-and-revision semantics

🌀 Mentaury Soul
cognition / self / identity / goals / values / feelings / relationships / becoming

🪁 Mentaury-Kernel
cross-domain semantic composition
provenance / scope / authority / declared loss / compatibility / non-escalation
```

This separation is an **ownership refinement**, not evidence that the original broader vision never existed.

## 5. Current Mentaury-Kernel role remains unchanged

The current architecture is still:

> **Mentaury-Kernel = technology-neutral composition specification for safe semantic interoperability between independently governed domains.**

It does not become a third cognition system, identity engine, truth authority or runtime coordinator merely because its historical origin was broader.

The founder-origin record is useful because it explains *why* the project family contains Native Kernel, Mentaury Soul and Mentaury-Kernel, while the present ownership model explains *where each responsibility lives now*.
